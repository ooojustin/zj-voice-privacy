zj-voice privacy policy

Effective September 10, 2026

zj-voice is a private voice-control client for AI coding agents. This policy describes how the zj-voice Android app and its companion service operated by Justin Garofolo handle information.

Information processed

  • Microphone audio. When you start a voice session or answer a voice prompt, the app captures audio and streams it to your configured zj-voice service. The service sends that audio to OpenAI to transcribe speech and generate responses.
  • Commands and agent activity. The service processes transcripts, requested actions, task state, agent responses, and delivery acknowledgements so it can act on a request and report the outcome.
  • Technical identifiers and diagnostics. The app and service process random client, session, operation, and event identifiers, connection state, timestamps, app version, and error information needed to authenticate the client, resume operations, prevent duplicate actions, and diagnose failures.
  • Configuration and credentials. The app stores the private service address and authentication credentials that you configure. Credentials are stored in app-private storage and protected with Android Keystore encryption where supported by the control connection.

The app does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or marketing.

How information is used and disclosed

Information is used only to provide, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot zj-voice. Audio and the associated request content are disclosed to OpenAI as a service provider for transcription, language processing, and synthesized speech. OpenAI handles that data under its applicable business and API data terms. The service is hosted on infrastructure controlled by the developer. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

Storage and retention

zj-voice does not intentionally retain raw microphone audio after streaming and processing complete. OpenAI may retain API data according to the account's data-control settings and its applicable policies. The companion service retains command text, task outcomes, and provenance needed to explain and recover operations for up to 30 days, subject to a 1 GiB storage cap. Security and operational records may be retained longer where needed to protect the service or comply with law.

On-device configuration, encrypted credentials, cached agent state, and pending-operation records remain until they are replaced, cleared by the app, or removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Security

The app is designed to connect only to a private companion service across an encrypted NetBird network. Credentials are kept in Android app-private storage and Android Keystore is used for control credentials. No security measure eliminates all risk.

Your choices and deletion

Microphone access can be revoked in Android settings. You can stop collection by ending a voice session. Clearing app storage or uninstalling zj-voice removes its on-device data. To request deletion of data retained by the companion service, email jgarofolo.dev@gmail.com. Requests are verified to prevent unauthorized deletion and completed unless retention is required for security or legal obligations.

Children

zj-voice is not directed to children and is intended only for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
